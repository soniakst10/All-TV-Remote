All Tv Remote Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how All Tv Remote (package com.rsbdsoft.tvremote) handles information when you use the app. The app is provided by rsbdsoft.

Information handled on your device

The app stores your selected TV name and network address, connection preferences, and pairing information on your device. The Android TV client identity is protected using Android Keystore. The app can also save a crash diagnostic and pairing stage locally so you can view, copy, or clear it in the app.

Local network and infrared

To discover, pair with, and control a compatible Android TV, the app communicates directly with that TV over your local network. Connection and pairing messages are sent to the TV you select; they are not sent to the app developer. On compatible phones, infrared commands are transmitted directly by the phone.

Collection, sharing, and advertising

The app does not use advertising or analytics and does not send personal data or crash reports to the developer. It does not create an app account. Information kept by the app remains on your device unless you choose to copy or share a diagnostic yourself, or your device platform handles data under its own settings.

Security and retention

Android TV connections use the TV remote protocol's TLS connection and the app pins the paired TV certificate. Locally stored app data remains until you remove it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can clear the saved crash diagnostic from the connection panel.

Children

All Tv Remote is a general-purpose remote utility and is not designed for children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's practices change. The latest version will be published at this page. For privacy questions, contact rsbdsoft@gmail.com.
